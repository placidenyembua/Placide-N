My Story

My journey is driven by curiosity, continuous learning and a desire to create meaningful solutions. Originally from the Democratic Republic of the Congo,(Gandajika) I am currently based in Kenya (Nairobi), where I continue to develop my professional and technical skills.

My interests bring together technology, communication, business and creativity. Whether building digital experiences, helping people communicate across languages or supporting data operations, I approach every opportunity with professionalism and dedication.

I believe that growth comes from embracing different experiences, staying adaptable and creating value through collaboration.

My long-term vision is to become a well-rounded professional who contributes to meaningful projects, develops innovative digital solutions, and creates value for individuals, businesses, and communities. I believe that success comes from continuous learning, consistency, embracing different experiences, and having the courage to turn ideas into reality.
